FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions regarding online assessment procedures, scoring, and certificates.

Is QuizPro free to use for students? ▼
Yes! QuizPro is 100% open-source and free for all students, faculty, and departments across all university courses.
How do I download my completion certificate? ▼
After achieving a passing score (50%+), you can view, print, and download your official verified PDF certificate directly from the result page or student dashboard.
Can I retake an assessment if I want to improve my score? ▼
Yes! You can retake any subject quiz unlimited times. Your highest score and accuracy will reflect on your profile and the honor leaderboard.
Is QuizPro accessible on mobile phones and tablets? ▼
Yes, QuizPro is completely responsive with touch-optimized interfaces, smooth left-sliding mobile menus, and full-screen examination modes on all screen sizes.
